# NLP Project	

The supermarket chain **Good Seed** would like to explore whether Data Science can help them adhere to alcohol laws by ensuring they do not sell alcohol to underage individuals. You are asked to evaluate this possibility using photos of individuals along with their labeled ages.

## Context:
- Stores are equipped with cameras at checkout counters triggered when a person is buying alcohol.
- Computer vision techniques will be used to estimate a person’s age based on a photograph.
- The goal is to develop and evaluate a model that can verify a person’s age.

# Data Description

You’ll work with a dataset of **photographs and labeled ages**. The objective is to train a computer vision model that can predict age.

Key characteristics:
- **Images**: Headshots of individuals.
- **Labels**: Real ages of individuals in the photos.

Model training and evaluation must be performed on a **GPU platform** due to high computational needs.
